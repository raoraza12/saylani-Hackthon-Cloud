# CFO Findings - Indus Weave Textiles

## Finding 1 - Paid overtime is not worked overtime, and the gap is widening
Over April-September 2026, overtime computed from biometric punches totalled 29,116 hours, while approved and paid overtime totalled 36,402 hours - a gap of 7,286 hours. The gap grew from 856 hours in April to 1,748 hours in September, while worked overtime stayed flat at 4,670-5,051 hours a month, yet the overtime bill rose from PKR 2.81 million in April to PKR 3.48 million in September (+23.5%). Faisalabad Plant accounts for the largest share, with 14,418 paid hours against 9,895 computed hours. Evidence: `analytics/q05_overtime_compare.sql` and `analytics/q06_paid_exceeds_computed.sql` (74 department-months exceed computed overtime by more than 15%). Recommended action: pay overtime only from computed biometric overtime, and require a computed-overtime check before any approval, starting with Faisalabad.

## Finding 2 - Approvals are paying for days with no overtime worked
Of 19,666 approved overtime requests, 3,518 (17.9%) had zero computed overtime on that day, including 584 Present days where the employee did not work past shift end and the remainder on Absent, Missing Punch, On Leave, Holiday or Weekly Off days. This is not a workload increase; it is an approval control failure, because payroll pays from approved requests rather than from punches. Evidence: `analytics/q05_overtime_compare.sql`, joined to `fact_attendance_daily` on employee and ot_date, and `analytics/q06_paid_exceeds_computed.sql`. Recommended action: block overtime approval when computed overtime for that employee and date is zero, and audit all 3,518 requests for recovery or correction.

## Finding 3 - Payroll did not stop when employees left
Five employees continued to receive pay after their separation date, totalling PKR 1,983,188 across 21 employee-months. The largest case is Umair Baloch (E10471), separated on 23 April 2026, who was paid May-September 2026 totalling PKR 682,100, and who is also the only employee-month in the data with pay and zero Present days. Evidence: `analytics/q08_paid_after_separation.sql` and `analytics/q07_paid_zero_attendance.sql`. Recommended action: make separation_date automatically stop payroll in the following month, and run the Q8 check every payroll run until the five cases are recovered and closed.
