What to fix first

Biggest absolute drop: Screened step, 718 applications lost. Biggest percentage drop: Offered step, only 34.2% moved forward. Screening and interview capacity are the bottleneck, not the number of applications.

Median time to hire is 33 days. Every stage takes about 8 to 9 days on median, so no single stage is unusually slow. The loss happens at the decision, not in waiting time.
